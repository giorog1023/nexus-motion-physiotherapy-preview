Pass 2 V9: Two-Tone Cyan-to-Mint Dimensional Glass Lighting

Pass 2 V8 established the approved watertight 3D solid geometry baseline (0.136 world units thickness, 8-segment front/rear bevels, continuous corner arcs). Pass 2 V9 delivers the definitive optical lighting correction from the Original Reference: **cool icy cyan-blue on the left perimeter (#1fcfeb), a natural organic turquoise connecting bridge (#26e0cc), and fresh luminous mint-green on the right perimeter (#34d399)**. The lighting is strictly attached to card-local 3D geometry coordinates and surface normals, creating differentiated optical responses across the front bevel, left/right side walls, top/bottom rails, and all four rounded corners. The artificial double-parallel stripe artifact from V8 has been eliminated in favor of a unified curvature-driven Fresnel and specular glaze. V8 solid geometry, Pass 2 V6 active frosted pearl face, Pass 2 V7 inactive cyan-blue glass, Pass 1 V19 typography, spine, camera, and particles remain 100% frozen.
