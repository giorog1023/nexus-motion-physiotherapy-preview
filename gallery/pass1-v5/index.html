1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & RESOLUTION ARCHITECTURE

Systematic resolution of the two external review directives: calibrating the visible proportions of the top identifier and details link to match the benchmark's exact 35.7% ratio, and eliminating hard dark outline circles to restore soft, luminous, feathered bokeh particles.

PARTICLE RENDERING REPAIR (SOFT BOKEH RESTORED)
• Dark Outline Root Cause: In GlobalComposite.fs, the previous shader included smoothstep(0.04, 0.09, max(scene.g, scene.b)). When particles feathered down below 0.04 brightness at their outer perimeters, they were abruptly reclassified as solid objects (solidObject = 1.0). This threw away the glowing studio background and substituted the near-black scene buffer, cutting a 2–4px pitch-black ring around every particle.
• Permanent Architectural Fix: Removed the arbitrary lower-bound brightness cutoff. The chromatic saturation ratio (G + B) / (2 * max(R, 0.0001)) remains ≥ 25.0 across the entire particle disc all the way down to complete transparency, enabling seamless additive Gaussian falloff into the background.
• Distribution & Palette: Extended horizontal spread from ±4.25 to ±7.25 around the spine, and calibrated palette to 40% emerald/mint green, 30% turquoise, and 30% cyan-blue (~28–35 visible elements).
TYPOGRAPHY PROPORTION CALIBRATION (BENCHMARK MATCH)
• Empirical Benchmark Ratios: Direct pixel measurement of the original reference active card demonstrated that both the top badge text and bottom details link are exactly 35.7% of the capital title letter height.
• Top Anatomical Identifier: Increased from 44px to 64px (+35% letter height, ratio 0.355), with a 98px height capsule, 2.4px thin grey border (rgba(85, 105, 125, 0.45)), and refined grey color (#3b4d60). Readability is commanding without zooming.
• Main Treatment Title: Increased from 172px to 180px (+4.6%) and visual weight modestly stepped to 650 (semibold-plus), preserving solid dark-navy (#0a1628) and crisp antialiasing.
• Bottom Details Link: Increased from 48px to 64px (+33%, ratio 0.355) with 68px delicate arrow and refined grey color (#263646).
