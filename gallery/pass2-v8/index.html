Pass 2 V8: Authentic Three-Dimensional Glass Solid Construction

In Pass 2 V7, the cards relied on flat thin geometry (thickness 0.0133 world units) with a 2D cookie-cutter SDF fragment-discard and a simple rim outline, producing dark flat rectangular slabs at oblique angles. Pass 2 V8 introduces a genuine watertight 2-manifold rounded beveled extrusion (thickness 0.136 world units, ~10.2× thicker, calibrated directly to Cards 02 and 03 in the Original Reference). The solid features an 8-segment smooth circular front bevel, modeled 4-segment top, bottom, and side surfaces, an 8-segment rear bevel, and a flat rear cap. Curvature is continuous across all 4 rounded corners throughout the entire 3D depth. The entire 14-treatment carousel shares a single geometry buffer instance with zero memory bloat, preserving 100% of Pass 1 V19 typography, Pass 2 V6 active glass, and Pass 2 V7 inactive glass.
